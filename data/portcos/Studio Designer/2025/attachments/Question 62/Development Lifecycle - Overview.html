13. Development Lifecycle
1
Natalia Śmietanowska
Feb 26

Knowing and understanding the Development Life-cycle is important for every team member. It describes how software is developed and released to end users in repeated iterations.

The diagram above may seem complex but it summarizes the timeline of release well. It does not describe and present very detailed activities, but it serves the purpose of explaining the development life-cycle.

Locate the all green marks on diagram - these are represent all activities that happen within a sprint. The sprint can be broken in 3 stages:

  • Stage 1 - Planning and Grooming

  • Stage 2 - Development and Testing

  • Stage 3 - Release

Stage 1 Planning and Grooming - Planning and Grooming starts before the sprint itself, because in Stage 1 Product Owners and the team prepare for the sprint. They groom the tasks, check if the Acceptance Criteria are complete, prioritize backlog, and prepare the sprint contents. Grooming is the first activity performed in this stage.

After the tickets are groomed they are shared to the team in the form of slack channels - one per individual task, for the team to perform the planning. Planning includes estimations that are provided by all team members on those slack channels. Estimations are obligatory and aid Product Owners in calculating the workload for each sprint. These slack channels are also used to answer all questions that testing and development may have pertaining to implementing each task.

Once the sprint contents are defined, the Sprint starts. At Studio Designer we work in 2 week sprint life-cycle. Which means that development and testing have 2 weeks to complete the work. At the end of that 2 weeks Sprint Certification is occurring. This means that the Release team reviews Patch Notes which will be delivered to clients, and the code patch that will be merged to Production branch is being prepared.

Generally when one sprint ends, another simultaneously starts - hence “life-cycle”. Thanks to this model of developing software we are able to provide new software increments overtime.
